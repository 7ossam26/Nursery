# Billing agreements and recurring generation

Phase 14 adds an agreement layer above the Phase 13 immutable obligations. An agreement is drafted with a mode (`MONTHLY`, `FIXED`, or `ADDITIONAL`), category, family normal/agreed total, selected child IDs, service terms, and either a monthly due day or a fixed installment schedule. Drafts create no obligation or treasury row. Approval stores the allocation snapshot and creates the first eligible period immediately.

Equal tuition allocation uses integer piastres. The agreed family total is divided by the stable ascending child UUID order; leftover piastres go to the first children in that order. Fixed installment amounts are split with a rotating stable remainder so each child’s stored installment rows sum to its stored allocation. Historical rows are never recomputed when siblings change. An optional first-period agreed amount is stored separately for a monthly agreement; it is an explicit amount and does not perform proration.

Monthly due dates are Cairo date-only values. Day 29–31 clamps to the last day of the month, including February in leap and non-leap years. The occurrence key is `(agreement_id, child_id, service_period_start, category_id)` and is unique in PostgreSQL. The worker locks children in UUID order and then the agreement; it advances at most 24 periods per transaction and can be safely restarted. pg-boss queue `billing-monthly-v1` uses a Cairo `* * * * *` schedule and sends a startup sweep. Each job rechecks current finance-module and license state.

Pauses and future price versions apply only to future monthly periods. An end date prevents later generation while preserving issued obligations. Additional fees are one independent obligation even when they have a service period label. Parent blocks and child status changes do not mutate approved terms; archive ends future eligibility, and an intentional finance disable records periods as `DISABLED` without issuing debt. Re-enabling the module only resumes the worker; an administrator must review the exact stored period/child allocation preview and approve its hash. A replayed approval is idempotent and is re-authorized against current scope.

The administration billing workspace is bilingual and can be opened from account navigation. Child onboarding exposes the same workspace after account/child creation, while actual collection remains the Phase 15 action. Billing routes use `billing.manage` and the existing child/branch/classroom policy.
