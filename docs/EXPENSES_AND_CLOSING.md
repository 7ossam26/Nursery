## Implemented sources and authority

Migration `0014_spending.sql` follows the actual Phase 15 commit `274fc3c`. Adds immutable expense categories, expenses, approval/cancellation events, unique settlements, paired account transfers, private expense documents and reasoned retirement records. Nursery approval settings are versioned and audited. New delegable capabilities `expenses.manage`, `expenses.pay`, `expenses.approve` are not silently granted to existing roles.

Pending expense stores original branch/optional classroom, category identifying snapshot, positive integer-string amount, due date and note. Classroom must belong to the branch. Its amount is not an outflow. Approval/cancellation append events; paid expense cannot be cancelled or repaid. Manual sources are operating expenses; reserved SALARY/PAYROLL/ADVANCE category codes cannot masquerade as payroll source records. Payroll remains Phase 19.

Threshold `0` explicitly disables approval. Positive threshold requires a scoped approval with the currently configured nonreserved capability when the amount is strictly greater than the threshold; equality is allowed without approval. Approval and payment can be separate actors. Settings updates require branch-level `modules.manage`, strict expectedVersion and audit. A changed approving capability invalidates earlier approvals for settlement under that new capability. Payments validate actual account method, original expense branch, effective date, recorded funds and current actor authority.

`SpendingService` reuses `ChildService.withPolicy`, `FinancialCore.operation`, canonical `treasury_balances` and sorted treasury account locks. Expense payment locks settings, actor operation, expense and accounts; configuration takes settings before the operation to avoid inversion. Source, signed outflow, audit and durable retry result commit together. Transfer requires branch-level `treasury.manage` over both accounts, actual funding and equal opposite source/destination movements; source/leg uniqueness and deferred PostgreSQL constraints reject incomplete or mismatched postings. Both-side history permission prevents exposing an inaccessible destination. Transfers never enter expense paid totals.

`/api/v1/expenses`: options, list, create, categories, settings, `:id/{approve,cancel,pay,history,documents}`. `/api/v1/finance/transfers`: paired posting and scoped history. Lists/totals share the same branch/classroom/state predicate and are paginated. History uses business dates and source reasons/actors, omitting private audit timestamps.

`PrivateDocumentStore` extracted from existing child-document filesystem handling; the child service and expense service reuse identical root/symlink/key/integrity guards and the existing PDF/JPEG/PNG parser/re-encoder. Expense uploads additionally use actor operation identity; file bytes are hashed for that identity and omitted from audit JSON. Current finance/document capability, original expense scope and enabled Finance are checked on every list/download. Attachment is private/no-store; no storage paths or keys are exposed. Retirement retains referenced historical bytes. Failed transaction candidates are removed only after checking no DB reference; uncertain DB acknowledgement preserves candidates for conservative SYSTEM cleanup of old unreferenced validated UUID files.

Pages `/administration/{expenses,transfers}` provide bilingual cash-effect explanations, explicit actual account/payment confirmation, full filtered pending/paid totals, categories/threshold controls, history/documents and FinancialCore status lookup before identical uncertain retry. Shared controls and scoped reads reused. No browser automation or persisted offline mutation queue.
